A .txt file in Netscape format. It's saved as default.txt in this service's cookie folder, whatever it was called.

What this service has

A download can go through your VPN provider's proxy servers: nothing to install, and only the jobs you switch it on for go that way. Everything else uses your normal connection.

Provider
NordVPN login

NordVPN's proxies need its service credentials, not your email and password. Find them at Nord Account, NordVPN, Set up NordVPN manually.

Preferred servers (optional)

To always use one server in a country, give its number: id69.nordvpn.com is country id, server 69.

Surfshark login

Use the service credentials from your Surfshark account (VPN, Manual setup), not your email and password.

Proxy per country

The provider needs HTTP or HTTPS proxy servers. A VPN that only offers WireGuard or OpenVPN, or only SOCKS proxies, can't be used this way. Leave a password empty to keep the saved one.

Test the connection

Connects through the saved VPN once and asks which country websites see.

How to use it

On the Download page, turn the VPN switch on and choose a country, then check or download. Pin the show and it keeps going through that country, while other shows use your normal connection. You can change a pinned show's route on the Queue tab.
